Cancer Treatment Options

💊 Chemotherapy

Chemotherapy uses powerful drugs to destroy cancer cells or slow their growth. It may be used alone or in combination with other treatments.

☢ Radiation Therapy

Radiation therapy uses high-energy radiation to destroy cancer cells and shrink tumors. It is commonly used for localized cancers.

🩺 Surgery

Surgery involves physically removing cancerous tissue from the body. It is often used when cancer is confined to a specific area.

🧬 Immunotherapy

Immunotherapy helps the body's immune system recognize and fight cancer cells more effectively.

🎯 Targeted Therapy

Targeted therapy focuses on specific genes or proteins that contribute to cancer growth, reducing damage to healthy cells.

📋 Treatment Selection

The choice of treatment depends on the type of cancer, stage of disease, patient health condition and medical recommendations from healthcare professionals.

Important Note

Treatment outcomes can vary widely among individuals. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider to determine the most appropriate treatment plan for your specific situation.
