❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can cancer be cured?

Some cancers can be successfully treated, especially when detected early.

Does every tumor mean cancer?

No. Tumors can be benign or malignant.

🥗 Nutrition Guidance

A balanced diet containing fruits, vegetables, proteins and whole grains helps support overall health during cancer treatment and recovery.

💙 Emotional Support

Cancer can affect mental health. Emotional support from family, friends and healthcare professionals is important during treatment.

📚 Educational Resources

Learn about cancer types, symptoms, treatments and screening methods through trusted medical resources.

🩺 Follow-Up Care

Regular follow-up visits help monitor recovery and identify any recurrence of disease.

📞 Patient Support Services

Many hospitals provide counseling, rehabilitation and support programs for cancer patients and caregivers.
