Cancer Symptoms

🎗 Breast Cancer Symptoms

  • Breast lump or thickening
  • Changes in breast size or shape
  • Nipple discharge
  • Skin dimpling or irritation
  • Pain in the breast area

🫁 Lung Cancer Symptoms

  • Persistent cough
  • Chest pain
  • Shortness of breath
  • Coughing blood
  • Unexplained weight loss

⚠ Warning Signs Requiring Medical Attention

  • Persistent unexplained pain
  • Unusual bleeding
  • Rapid unexplained weight loss
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Long-lasting fatigue

Important Note

Having one or more symptoms does not necessarily mean a person has cancer. These symptoms may also be caused by other medical conditions. Consult a healthcare professional for proper diagnosis.
